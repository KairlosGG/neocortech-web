  • VR · Inclusive training

    LiVRe

    ChallengeHelp people with intellectual disabilities practise everyday tasks in virtual reality and gain independence, at their own pace and without fear of getting it wrong.

    Where
    9 care centres for people with disabilities across Córdoba, Seville and Málaga provinces. Client: Fundación Magtel (Futuro Singular programme).
    How we tested it
    Around 20 people with very different profiles tried both controllers and hand tracking, in alternating order.
    What we changed
    Hand tracking by default, with controllers as an alternative for people with less mobility; more colour and contrast so objects are easy to tell apart; visual and audio feedback on every action, so people always know when they have got it right.
    Design safeguards
    No artificial movement, so nobody feels dizzy; one instruction at a time; mistakes carry no penalty and can be retried.
    Outcome
    A project worth over €100,000; each person’s progress was tracked on a data platform.
    My role: project direction and user testing in care centres.

  • Web360 · Virtual reality · Industrial heritage

    Cerco Industrial · Phase III

    ChallengeDocument and share the industrial heritage of the Cerco in Peñarroya-Pueblonuevo, Spain, both on site and from anywhere.

    Starting data
    Around 13,900 aerial and ground photographs to rebuild more than twenty buildings in 3D, optimised to run in real time on the web and in virtual reality.
    Design decisions
    A minimal interface with pictograms and a clear hierarchy; numbered points for each building with its own card; then-and-now comparisons; narration in Spanish and English.
    At the viewpoint
    A virtual reality station with six swivel seats, so people can look around in 360º without walking, six standalone headsets and tablets so staff can guide each visitor.
    Loads anywhere
    Each building loads separately and there is a light version for less powerful devices.
    Outcome
    Phase III published, funded by the EU LEADER programme through the Valle del Alto Guadiato rural development group. Outside EONESIA.
    My role: designed and directed part of the project, together with a partner.

07 The new layer · AI governance

Agents already decide on their own. Their logs record what they did, but not why. I work in that gap.

AGENTIC SYSTEMOPAQUE Hover or press and hold to see the flow
The core of my work

I design AI systems that can be audited.

This box is an agentic system as a committee sees it today: a request goes in, a decision comes out, and in between, fog. Hence the question mark.

Open it: hover or press and hold. The opaque part reveals its flow — perception, reasoning, applied policy, action — and stops being a black box. With ISO/IEC 42001 as the framework, that traceability is designed into the system, not bolted on.

74% of companies plan to deploy agentic AI within the next two years, but only 21% have a mature governance model for autonomous agents (Deloitte, State of AI in the Enterprise, 2026 ↗).
